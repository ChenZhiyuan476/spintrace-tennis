Tennis racket hit samples
=========================

Source: "Tennis Ball being hit" by jacklilley
Source page: https://freesound.org/people/jacklilley/sounds/338122/
Description: Tennis balls being hit by a professional, recorded with a Rode
microphone and Zoom H4N.
License: Creative Commons Zero 1.0 (CC0 / public domain dedication)
License page: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

tennis_hit_1.wav, tennis_hit_2.wav and tennis_hit_3.wav are short, normalized,
mono excerpts prepared from the public preview recording. Attribution is not
required by CC0, but the source is retained here for provenance.
